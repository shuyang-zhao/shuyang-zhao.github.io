I'm Shuyang Zhao (you can also call me Noah), a second-year Ph.D. student in Chemistry at the University of California, Riverside, working in [Prof. Yinsheng Wang's lab](https://wanglab.ucr.edu). My doctoral research focuses on the chemical biology of RNA modifications, epitranscriptomics, and engineering artificial intelligence for biological research.

Before coming to Riverside, I earned a B.Pharm. in Pharmaceutical Science from the School of Medicine at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://cuhk.edu.cn), with training in drug delivery, immunotherapy, organic chemistry and analytical chemistry. Along the way I worked on biomaterials and drug delivery with [Prof. Zhaoting Li](https://zhaotingli.com) and [Prof. Wing-Fu Lai](https://environment.leeds.ac.uk/faculty/staff/11935/dr-wing-fu-lai) at CUHK-Shenzhen, on chemical biology and peptide chemistry with [Dr. Mao Li](https://www.x-mol.com/groups/limao) at Shenzhen Bay Laboratory, and, in the summer of 2025, on base editing with [Prof. Chengqi Yi](https://www.yilab.org.cn) at Peking University. I am grateful to each of them for their guidance.

If you are also interested in RNA modifications or AI for biological research, feel free to write to me at shuyang.zhao[at]email.ucr.edu.
