This policy covers **s886-rclone**, a Google OAuth client used only by the site owner (Shuyang Zhao) to back up personal research files from a personal server to the owner's own Google Drive.

- The app requests only the `drive.file` scope, which limits access to files the app itself created.
- It is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.
- No data is collected, stored, sold, or shared with third parties. Files go directly from the owner's server to the owner's Google Drive.
